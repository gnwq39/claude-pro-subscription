## Which option should you choose?

### Choose Claude Free if you use Claude occasionally

The free plan is the simplest option because it requires no subscription payment. It is suitable for occasional writing, brainstorming, short coding questions, and trying Claude before committing to a paid tier.

It becomes less attractive when you repeatedly hit usage limits, need longer work sessions, or want access to paid features such as Claude Code and expanded project workflows.

Pro is the right starting point for most individual users. It gives you more usage and paid features without the $100 or $200 monthly cost of Max.

Max 5x is aimed at users who work with Claude frequently and need substantially more capacity. Developers, researchers, and people who use Claude for long coding or document sessions may find the extra allowance useful.

Max 20x costs $200 per month, the same as the official annual total for Pro. It is designed for people who use Claude heavily throughout the day and need the highest individual usage tier.

For casual users, it is difficult to justify. The plan makes more sense when interruptions have a direct cost, such as delayed development work, research deadlines, or extensive daily collaboration with Claude.

## Common problems after payment

### The subscription has not appeared yet

Wait for the stated processing window first. Refresh Claude, sign out and back in, then verify the email address and current plan.

Do not submit another payment while the first order is still processing. WildAI specifically warns users not to pay twice during the activation period.

### The account still shows Free

Check whether you are signed in to the same Claude account used during the order. If the account is correct, keep the order number, payment confirmation, and Claude billing screenshot ready for support.

WildAI’s troubleshooting documentation recommends checking the account email, order status, and current plan before contacting support.

### Cookie validation fails

Common causes include:

- Exporting cookies from a page where Claude was not logged in
- Copying incomplete JSON
- Using a stale or expired cookie
- Submitting data from the wrong Claude account
- Having an active subscription or overdue billing state
- Changing network locations repeatedly

If you already have an active or overdue Claude subscription, the provider’s troubleshooting page says you may need to restore the account to the Free state before attempting another subscription flow.

### You are worried about account security

Stop the process if the page asks for more information than you expected or if the domain does not match the service you intended to use. A subscription is not worth handing over credentials to an unknown website.

After the order is complete, review active sessions and account security settings. If you no longer trust the session that was used, sign out everywhere or take the account-security action available in Claude.
